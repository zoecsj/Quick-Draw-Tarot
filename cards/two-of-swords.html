Minor Arcana

Two of Swords

Clarity appears when you pause and face what you already know.

Keywords

Stalemate · Choice · Boundaries · Balance · Insight

Upright

You are weighing options carefully and protecting your peace while you decide. A balanced perspective will reveal the path forward.

Reversed

Avoidance or indecision may be creating extra tension. Name the truth directly so momentum can return.

Reflection

What truth are you ready to acknowledge so your next choice feels clear?
