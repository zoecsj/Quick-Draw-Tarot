Minor Arcana · Cups

King of Cups

Emotional maturity—steady leadership with a calm heart.

Keywords

Balance · Compassion · Stability · Wisdom · Diplomacy

Upright

Stay calm and compassionate. You can hold emotion without being ruled by it.

Reversed

Suppressed feelings or volatility. Practice honest expression and regulation.

Reflection

Where do you need to respond with calm instead of reacting?
