Nine of Pentacles

Self-sufficiency—comfort earned and independence enjoyed.

Keywords

Independence · Comfort · Abundance · Confidence · Reward

Upright

Enjoy what you’ve built. Your discipline is paying off in real ways.

Reversed

Imposter feelings or dependence. Reclaim confidence in what you’ve earned.

Reflection

Where have you already succeeded but won’t let it count?
