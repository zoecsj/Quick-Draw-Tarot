Knight of Pentacles

Steady progress—reliability, routine, and long-term effort.

Keywords

Consistency · Routine · Responsibility · Persistence · Practicality

Upright

Keep going—slow, steady effort wins. Build with patience and discipline.

Reversed

Stagnation or stubbornness. Adjust the routine and re-energize the plan.

Reflection

What small daily action would move you forward over time?
