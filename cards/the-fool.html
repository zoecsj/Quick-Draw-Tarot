Major Arcana

The Fool

A leap of faith invites you into a fresh chapter.

Keywords

Beginnings · Freedom · Curiosity · Trust · Adventure

Upright

The Fool encourages you to move forward with openness and wonder. Progress comes from trusting your inner yes.

Reversed

You may be hesitating out of fear or rushing without grounding. Pause long enough to align your risk with your values.

Reflection

What new path are you ready to step onto, even without every answer?
