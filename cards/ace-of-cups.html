Minor Arcana

Ace of Cups

Emotional renewal opens when you receive life with softness.

Keywords

Love · Intuition · Healing · Openness · Compassion

Upright

Your heart is ready for a sincere beginning in love, creativity, or spiritual connection. Let tenderness lead your next step.

Reversed

Emotional overwhelm or guardedness may be blocking your flow. Make space to process your feelings before reaching outward.

Reflection

Where can you allow more emotional honesty to refill your inner cup?
