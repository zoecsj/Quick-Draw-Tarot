Minor Arcana · Cups

Nine of Cups

Contentment—wishes, pleasure, and emotional satisfaction.

Keywords

Satisfaction · Joy · Gratitude · Fulfillment · Comfort

Upright

Enjoy what you’ve earned. Let yourself receive and savor the moment.

Reversed

Hedonism or emptiness. Check if you’re chasing comfort over meaning.

Reflection

What do you truly want—and what would “enough” feel like?
