Three of Cups

Joy shared—friendship, support, and community that lifts you.

Keywords

Friendship · Celebration · Community · Support · Joy

Upright

Connect and celebrate. Let your people refill you.

Reversed

Drama or burnout. Protect your peace and choose your circle wisely.

Reflection

Who makes you feel safe to be fully yourself?
