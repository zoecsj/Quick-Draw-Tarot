Four of Pentacles

Holding tight—security, control, and fear of loss.

Keywords

Security · Control · Stability · Possessiveness · Conservation

Upright

Protect what matters, but don’t suffocate it. Balance saving with living.

Reversed

Loosening grip. Release scarcity thinking and allow flow.

Reflection

Where are you holding on too tightly—and what are you afraid will happen?
