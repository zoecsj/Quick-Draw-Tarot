How to Read Tarot

Tarot is not about predicting the future.

It’s about revealing perspective.

Each card is a mirror.

The spread is the structure.

You are the interpreter.

Approach your reading with curiosity, not fear.

1 — Set an Intention

Pause before you draw.

Ask a clear question:

What do I need to understand right now?

What energy am I bringing into this?

What would help me move forward wisely?

Clarity in the question shapes clarity in the answer.

2 — Notice Your First Reaction

Before reading the meaning, ask:

What stands out?

What emotion surfaces?

Does this confirm something I already sensed?

Tarot works best as a conversation, not a verdict.

3 — Read the Whole Picture

A single card gives insight.

A spread shows patterns.

Look for:

Repeated suits

Major Arcana (big shifts)

Emotional tone across positions

Tarot shows direction — not destiny.

Choosing a Spread

Daily Reading (1 Card)
For grounding and focus.

Past / Present / Future
For understanding momentum.

Hermit’s Guidance
For self-reflection and inner clarity.

Clarity Spread
When you feel overwhelmed.

Decision Spread
When choosing between two paths.

Shadow Work
When something triggers you or repeats.

Year Ahead
For seasonal themes and long-term focus.

Choose the spread that matches the depth of your question.

If You Pull a “Bad” Card

There are no bad cards.

There are endings.

There are wake-up calls.

There are uncomfortable truths.

But there are no curses.

Pause.

Ask:

What is this showing me?

What needs to change?

What is trying to end so something healthier can begin?

Cards like The Tower, Death, or Ten of Swords often signal transformation — not disaster.

Tarot reflects energy.

It does not trap you in fate.

Awareness gives you choice.

A Gentle Reminder

Tarot is a reflective tool.

It is not a replacement for professional care, emergency support, or real-world help.

If a reading increases fear, hopelessness, or distress:

Step away.

Take a breath.

Talk to someone you trust.

Seek real support.

Your safety and wellbeing matter more than any card.

Tarot should help you feel clearer — not smaller.
